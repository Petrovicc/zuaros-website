MemoSpin

Remember the board. Find the pairs. Keep your focus as the game turns.

MemoSpin combines memory puzzles with a Solo journey and online multiplayer. Progress through Solo stages, play a private Duel with friends, or compete in team matches, free-for-all games and tournaments.

Your Google Play Games account

You can play as a guest or connect Google Play Games in MemoSpin. Linking your account helps you recover your existing game profile and progress when you sign in again on another installation.

Google Play Games supplies your player identity and profile information for sign-in. Your nickname and avatar can be used as initial profile defaults; custom choices you make in MemoSpin are preserved. MemoSpin does not ask for your Google password.
